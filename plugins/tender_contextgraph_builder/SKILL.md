# Tender ContextGraph Builder

Candidate-only fixed-DOCX extraction review and graph construction. All writes
remain append-only checkpoints under the authenticated workspace session boundary.

This capability cannot admit, promote, approve, or certify authority.

## Structured AI grounding

ContextGraph AI Ground may use the host-owned `PluginLlm` surface under this
plugin identity. It always inherits the current agent's active provider, model,
credentials, and trust policy; it must not request provider, model, profile, or
agent overrides. Structured output is candidate-only and must be validated
against canonical evidence anchors before persistence.

AI Ground cannot create Human Grounding events, GraphDeltas, releases,
activation snapshots, or semantic authority. Provider failure is explicit and
must not fall back to extraction-confidence threshold classification. Replay and
audit verification consume persisted, hashed results and never call the model.
