# Progressive depth and recurrent routing

Route by what must be delivered, not by words like “ethics” or “organization” alone. Do not compete with the host's native first-hop skill discovery. This is *in-skill* routing after the user has already selected Semantier Thinking.

## Levels and observable exit gates

| Depth | Enter when | Checks to run | Stop / transition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L0 direct | Clear, bounded task and no meaningful reasoning uncertainty | Complete task without checklist; retain consequential qualification | Task done; no extra skill |
| L1 focused | Local ambiguity or one load-bearing claim at low/moderate stakes | Clarify the point, check its single strongest evidence/inference and answer | Gap resolved, or named new risk escalates to L2 |
| L2 structured | Multiple plausible explanations, competing options, material assumptions or disputed evidence | List key claims, source quality, assumptions, alternatives, likely consequences and next check | Defensible scoped conclusion, or named unresolved consequential gap escalates to L3 |
| L3 deep audit | Explicit full audit, high-consequence irreversibility, substantial contradictory evidence or serious source-integrity concern | Source-located claim graph, strongest alternatives, assumption and causal/quantitative audit where relevant, normative/systemic lens when necessary, explicit unresolved unknowns | Auditable decision/report or clear bounded inability to determine; do not claim certainty by checklist completion |

**L3 does not mean always running 8×9 or 12×12 boxes**: explicitly scope which Paul–Elder elements/standards and Asking the Right Questions criteria are relevant. Record full applicable coverage when the user requests exhaustive auditing; mark N/A as not applicable, not pass.

## Mode — independent of depth

- **Deliver:** resolve everything possible with existing context, tools and available sources; ask the user only about a genuine blocking fact or preference. Deliver the requested result, including draft text if requested.
- **Coach:** ask a single high-information question, wait, diagnose the response, then move to the next discriminating question. Do not supply the user's answer by default. End with the basis of the user's judgment and a possible improvement, not a personality score.
- Never automatically convert Deliver to Coach to offload an assistant's research or avoid work.

## Progressive plan contract

At entry derive in working context (not a mandatory user-facing template):

- **Goal/finish condition:** what would count as a usable result?
- **Lead:** inquiry | decision | writing (or **NONE** for no material critical-thinking need).
- **Depth:** L0 | L1 | L2 | L3, with reason grounded in known stakes/evidence.
- **Mode:** deliver | coach; explicit user direction overrides default.
- **One open load-bearing gap:** what observation or user choice could change the result?
- **Next method:** smallest relevant workflow/reference or source check; only load specialist if its unique question is material.
- **Stop/upgrade:** what result closes this check? what concrete finding justifies more depth?

In ordinary exchanges keep this plan implicit; explain it briefly only for consequential, multi-step or requested audits. No second independent task ledger.

## Escalation triggers

A conflicting source, uncertainty that can flip a decision, a hard-to-reverse consequence, undefined value criterion, suspicious origin, causal inference unidentifiable from observed data, or organizational feedback beyond local causation. Escalate only the relevant branch; new levels are not a license to re-execute completed work.

## De-escalation and STOP

Stop if the material gap is resolved, user output is ready, new evidence is unavailable, more analysis is unlikely to alter the decision, or remaining uncertainty must be reported rather than inferred away. Offer bounded next verification instead of an endless literature search.

## Multi-skill composition

One primary skill; one specialist when necessary; both ethical and organizational only when two distinct questions genuinely matter. More than three concurrent methods is a routing smell. Prefer sequential handoff with provenance intact. Reuse facts once verified; do not silently promote *inferred* to *observed*.
