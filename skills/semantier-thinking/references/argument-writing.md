# Argument & Writing — inspect and deliver defensible communication

**Lead task:** critique, construct, revise, explain or write an argument or document. The requested finished text remains the deliverable unless the user expressly wants only analysis.

## L1 — finish a bounded draft
Clarify the central claim, audience and single most consequential evidential or logical issue. Fix that issue, honor the user's legitimately chosen position, and deliver complete usable wording. Do not add artificial debate sections.

## L2 — argument reconstruction and revision
1. State the issue and classify the question: fact, definition, causal explanation, evaluation/value or action/policy.
2. Distinguish **claim**, **grounds/evidence**, **warrant** (why the evidence bears on the claim), **qualifiers** and **exceptions/rebuttals** (Toulmin-inspired).
3. Identify load-bearing assumptions and the strongest reasonable alternative or objection; do not attribute unstated warrants to the author.
4. Check that claims match source scope, audience needs and factual confidence. Separate persuasion effectiveness from epistemic validity.
5. Draft/revise the complete text, preserving justified stance and qualifying unsupported universal, statistical or causal wording.

## L3 — source-grounded argument audit
- Build an **as-written** map using direct locators, then an **as-required** reconstruction for minimum bridges needed to support conclusions. Label reconstructed unsupported links explicitly.
- Distinguish defects: `GAP` (missing support), `LEAP` (inference unsupported), `ASSUMPTION`, `CONTRADICTION`, `SOURCE_LIMIT`, `UNRESOLVED` and `OPINION_DIFFERENCE` (not a logic defect). Do not label a fallacy without an anchored argument and explanation.
- Audit source freshness, attribution and provenance, strongest alternative and objections, scope and material implications. An argument diagram's completeness is not proof the argument is sound.
- Provide an actionable amended document or bounded audit, as actually requested. If the source is partially inaccessible, state coverage.

## Coach branch
Invite a user-authored provisional claim or evidence link before giving targeted feedback. For unassisted learning, compare the user's first judgment against externally verified material instead of having the model simply pronounce a grade. Do not insist on coaching when a direct draft is requested.

**No invention:** quotes, studies, precise numbers, stakeholders, citations, adverse findings, mental states and legal conclusions. A report may conclude that there is no demonstrated material defect within reviewed scope.
