# Method attribution, licensing and applicability

## Primary sources

- Richard Paul and Linda Elder, *Critical Thinking: Tools for Taking Charge of Your Professional and Personal Life* (Chinese edition: 《思辨与立场：生活中无处不在的批判性思维工具》, China Renmin University Press, 2016), especially chapters 3–7 (dispositions/elements/standards), 9 (decisions), 11–13 (group, moral and organizational reasoning), 14–15 (reflective practice). The 8 elements and 9 standards are analytical lenses, not a universally prescribed sequence.
- Peter A. Facione, *Critical Thinking: A Statement of Expert Consensus for Purposes of Educational Assessment and Instruction*, American Philosophical Association Delphi Report (1990): interpretation, analysis, evaluation, inference, explanation and self-regulation are a capability classification, not mandatory phases.
- Robert H. Ennis, *The Nature of Critical Thinking*: reasonable reflective judgment concerning what to believe or do.
- Stephen Toulmin, *The Uses of Argument* (1958): claims, grounds, warrants, qualifiers and rebuttals.
- Sam Wineburg and Sarah McGrew on civic online reasoning/lateral reading; Mike Caulfield's SIFT as a bounded online-source verification practice.
- Decision quality and reversibility approaches; problem structure, uncertainty, and consequences must be evaluated with field-specific evidence rather than formulaic scores.

## Epistemic and distribution limitations

- This skill is a designed synthesis, not an APA-certified workflow and not evidence that users' reasoning ability has improved.
- Scientific, legal, clinical, policy and organization claims require competent domain evidence; normative weights belong to authorized people.
- The Semantier Skills GitHub source is not the live signed Semantier Official registry. Official release identity is `semantier/semantier/semantier-thinking` with installed name `semantier--semantier-thinking`, but only a reviewed publisher signing key, a verified Ed25519 signature on a signed v1 catalog, immutable manifest-backed package and live OSS/HTTPS verification qualify it as an official distribution.
- If using external benchmarks, cite their dataset version, license, split and evaluation method; do not treat logic QA score as full critical-thinking competence.
